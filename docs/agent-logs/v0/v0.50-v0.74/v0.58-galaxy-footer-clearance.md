**How I did it**
- **Key point:** the galaxy canvas is fixed to the viewport (its parallax is only 0.025 × scroll), but the 375×812 page now scrolls 34 px. Text near the galaxy is therefore closest to it at two different scroll positions:
  - footer text: when scrolled to the end
  - panel text: at the top
  - So each band edge is now measured at its own worst scroll position.
- Measurements, page coordinates, 375 wide (`measure.mjs`, Playwright, glyph ranges):
  - © text top: 668, i.e. 178 px above the page bottom (dev-1's number is right). Links 702–726, eye button 742–774, switch 790–814.
  - Page height: 846 at every phone height up to 846, so max scroll is 34 at 812 and 179 at 667. At 414×896 the page fits (896) and © is again 178 px from the bottom.
  - Scrolled to the end, the page bottom is the viewport bottom. So the footer text is at `height - 178` on screen for every phone height, and `PHONE_BAND_BOTTOM = 186` (178 + 8).
- **Also found:** the panel's last text is now much lower than V0.39 measured. V0.51's real email wraps to two lines and the buttons take two rows, so LinkedIn ends at 549 (was ~495). On leader the galaxy already sat behind the LinkedIn button and the bottom of the panel. I updated `PHONE_BAND_TOP` to 557 (549 + 8) so the constant matches the page again.
- **Band too short for the galaxy:** with both edges correct, the band at 375×812 is only 69 px (557–626), and 100 px is the smallest galaxy that still reads as a galaxy (needs 128 px).
  - Before, the clamped galaxy was centred in the band, so it spilled over both edges, including the footer text.
  - Now it rests on the bottom edge (`cy = min(centre, bottom - 0.64 r)`): it always keeps the 8 px footer gap, and the overflow goes up behind the frosted contact panel instead.
  - I chose this over shrinking the galaxy to fit (r ≈ 54 at 812, too small to read as a galaxy). The footer text sits on the bare background since V0.50, while the panel is frosted glass.
