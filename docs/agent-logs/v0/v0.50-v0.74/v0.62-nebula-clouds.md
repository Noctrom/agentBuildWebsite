**How I did it**
- **Tileable noise:** `nebula.ts` has its own periodic value noise (`createTileNoise`: the lattice wraps every `px × py` cells, each octave twice the period). Every texture tiles exactly.
  - Each layer's canvas holds the tile plus wrapped copies to the right and below.
  - `draw` is one `drawImage` per layer with a source window at the wrapped offset.
  - The bilinear filter samples the copy past the window edge, so there are no seams.
  - Per frame: 2 full-screen draws (gas; banks with streamers), the starfield and 5 sprites. No noise is computed per frame. Before, it was 3 full-screen draws.
- **Banks:** 2 smooth octaves for the cloud masses plus 2 "billow" octaves (|2n − 1|) for the puffs, domain-warped, through a narrow threshold.
  - **Problem:** plain value noise lines its features up with its grid, and a sharp threshold turned that into boxy shapes and straight edges.
  - **Fix:** `offGrid` averages two lattices offset by half a cell, with the contrast restored. I use it for the puffs, the dust lanes and the gas.
- **Bake speed:** this was the main problem. My first full-screen version took about 1.3× as long as the S27 bake. What fixed it:
  - **Coarse grid:** smooth fields (warp, cloud masses, colour, dust lanes) are computed every 4th texel and interpolated (`sampleCoarse`).
  - **Streamers:** the upwind search runs once on the bank grid. Streamer texels are rejected cheaply if no bank is upwind.
  - **The biggest win:** V8 doesn't optimize a long loop inside a generator body, so the texel loops ran in the slow tiers. Each loop is now a plain function called once per row (`eachRow`), which the JIT optimizes after a few rows. That made the bake about 2× faster.
  - **Slices:** the bake yields every 600 texels of work, and the blur runs one line at a time. At 375 with 4× throttle the longest slices are the starfield setup at the start (about 11–14 ms, same as on leader). Bake slices are mostly under 5 ms.
  - **Writes:** one 32-bit write per texel, plus a table-based dither so smooth gradients don't band at full strength.
- **Full strength and DPR 2 (V0.61):** V0.61 merged into `leader` while I worked, so I tested in a scratch copy of the current `leader` with my `nebula.ts` (no merge on my branch).
  - At dpr ≥ 1.5 (the sharp hidden-content view on desktops and tablets) the banks bake at 2× the texels and get a finer, zero-mean octave of puffs. The normal view always bakes at 1×, so this never delays the first frame.
  - The engine rebuilds the scene in the background for that view; it takes about 190 ms at 1280×800 DPR 2.
  - The layer start offsets and young stars come from their own seeded generators on the normal-resolution grid, so both views show the same picture. Only the bank edges get finer in the sharp view.
  - The cache keeps the last two bakes (size × sharpness), so switching views back and forth doesn't bake again.
  - In the merged build at 1280×800 DPR 2 the canvas is 2560×1600 at opacity 1. Screenshots show no banding or blocky texels. The clouds are still soft by design.
- **Dropped:** a "near dust" layer drifting in front. It was nearly invisible and cost a full-screen draw. The dust lanes in the banks cover the "dark dust lanes" criterion.
- **Ambiguities, decided myself:**
  - The light comes from the upper left.
  - The drift is left and slightly up, with streamers trailing behind it.
  - The composition is seeded (the same on every load), not art-directed. Its banks land wherever the noise puts them.
