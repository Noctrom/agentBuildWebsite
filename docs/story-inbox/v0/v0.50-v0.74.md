# Approved stories V0.50–V0.74

### V0.50 — See-through footer with the Animation switch on the right
**Status:** in backlog · **Suggested owner:** dev-1 · **Depends on:** — (V0.41, V0.49 done) · **Drafted:** 2026-10-05

*As a visitor, I want the footer to blend into the space background, with the links and Animation switch in a clear order, so that the bottom of the page matches the see-through navigation.*

Today the footer is a frosted-glass band with a line along its top. Left to right it shows © name, the Animation switch (V0.41) in the middle, then GitHub, LinkedIn, Email. Chris wants it see-through like the V0.47 rail, with no line. He wants the Animation switch at the far right, and going right to left: Animation, Email, LinkedIn, GitHub. The copyright stays on the far left.
- [ ] The footer has no frosted or dark fill. The space background shows through it exactly as it does behind the page.
- [ ] No border line along the top of the footer
- [ ] From `sm` up, one row, left to right: © year + name on the far left, then GitHub, LinkedIn, Email, Animation switch, with the Animation switch at the far right edge of the content area
- [ ] Below `sm` (stacked, centered), top to bottom: © year + name, then GitHub, LinkedIn, Email, then the Animation switch
- [ ] Footer text, links and the switch pass WCAG AA contrast over the brightest background frame now that the glass is gone
- [ ] The switch's behaviour (V0.41, V0.42, V0.49) and the links' behaviour (new tab for GitHub/LinkedIn, mailto for Email) are unchanged. Keyboard tab order matches the visual order.
- [ ] Works at 375px, 768px and 1280px

**Open questions:** None.
