# V0.59 — Contact galaxy recoloured to match Chris's Milky Way photo (2026-10-07)
**Agent:** dev-2
**Branch:** story/v0.59-galaxy-palette · **Status:** done

**What I did**
- Core: a cream centre, then gold and amber, with burnt orange edges. It replaces the warm white. The breathing halo is gold too.
- Dust lanes: the lanes now tint what is left of the glow brown-black, so they read as dark dust on the gold. Before, they only thinned the glow.
- Outer arms: the arm glow runs from gold and amber inside, through a greyish mix (as in the photo), to blue-white and pale cyan at the tips. Arm stars inside 0.3 of the radius are warm gold. Outside it they are blue-white, with some pale cyan.
- Knots: rust instead of pink. They still twinkle as in V0.40: same live knots, same twinkle seeds.
- Sky (Contact only): an indigo-to-navy tint around the galaxy, many extra fine blue-white stars and 7 brighter blue-white stars, all in one layer baked per size. The starfield's two cloud colours on Contact are now navy and indigo (was one blue).

**How I did it**
- **Palette:** in `src/scenes/galaxyPalette.ts` (`MILKY_WAY` plus `mix`), so V0.60's band can reuse it. I sampled the photo's regions with a script, for example core median 169 118 67 / bright 230 181 114, outer band bright 152 174 218, and sky median 28 28 54. I scaled them up where the scene needs a light colour, because the canvas is shown at 0.2 opacity.
- **Galaxy:** all colour changes are baked once per resize:
  - disk gradient stops (`DISK_STOPS`)
  - 10 arm glow sprites along a radius ramp (`ARM_GLOW_RAMP`)
  - a rust knot sprite
  - a second dust pass with `source-atop` in dark brown
  - `STAR_COLORS` (now 6 entries)

  Arm star colour depends on radius, with a per-star hash spreading the boundary so it shows no ring. It uses no extra random draws, so the layout matches S27/V0.39.
- **Sky:**
  - **First try:** a navy tint over the whole screen. The AA scan showed that it lowered the text's worst contrast by 1–3% at every width (for example 7.17 → 6.96 at 1024). The story says contrast must be no worse, so I dropped it.
  - **Final:** the tint is an ellipse (`skyShape`) placed in the text-free area. I measured the text boxes on /contact at 768/1024/1280/1920:
    - the intro ends ~170 px from the top
    - the footer text starts 92 px above the bottom
  - **Tablet/desktop:** the ellipse reaches 0.75r up, 1.25r down, 1.3r left of the galaxy centre and off the right edge.
  - **Phones:** it covers only the band the galaxy already sweeps (V0.58), at full width.
  - **Stars:** the fine stars and bright stars are placed inside the ellipse too. Fine stars spread over the whole screen had also nudged two boxes down slightly.
  - **Drawing:** only the ellipse's bounding box is baked and drawn, one `drawImage` per frame. Nothing is redrawn per frame.
