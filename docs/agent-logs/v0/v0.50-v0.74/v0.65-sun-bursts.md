# V0.65 — About sun churns visibly and throws random sunbursts (2026-10-07)
**Agent:** dev-2
**Branch:** story/v0.65-sun-bursts · **Status:** done

**What I did**
- **Visible churn within 3 s:** the old granulation was two faint layers crossfading. It is now small bright cells with dark lanes that boil: each cell brightens and fades on its own phase, swells while it is bright and wanders a little. Over them, larger bright and dark swells drift across the disk, down and slightly left, at about 0.03 r/s. 38% of the visible disk's pixels change by at least 10/255 within 1 s, at both 375 and 1280. On leader the figure is 0%.
- **Size and position kept:** `layoutFor` is unchanged. The prominences, the flares and the breathing corona are unchanged. The prominences keep their exact shapes, because the bake skips the random numbers the old granulation used.
- **Sunburst:** first a bright patch on the limb flashes once: it rises for 0.4 s and is gone by 2 s. With it, a jet (a fan of glowing strands) or a loop (big plasma arches) shoots off the edge, grows for 1.4 s, then stretches outward. A glowing clump runs ahead of it, and everything fades out by the end of the burst (3.8–5 s).
- **Random timing:** each burst starts a random 20–40 s after the previous one started, and lasts at most 5 s, so two bursts never overlap. Its place on the visible limb is random, and so are its kind (jet or loop) and which way it leans. The visible arc is computed for each layout, at least 0.06 r inside the viewport: about 117°–228° at 1280×800 and 98°–194° at 375×812.
- **First burst:** it comes a random 20–40 s after the sun's first animated frame. Nothing happens on load.
- **AA at the brightest burst moment:** 0 failures at 375, 768, 1024 and 1280, with bursts forced through every edge position (numbers below).
- **No strobe:** see WCAG 2.3.1 under How.
- **Reduced motion / Animation switch off:** the still frame is time 0, and bursts are never drawn in a still frame. The canvas changes 0 channels, even in the test build with forced bursts.
- **No stutter:** the cost is close to leader's. See the numbers under Verification and the note under Follow-ups.
- **Other scenes unchanged:** only `src/scenes/sun.ts` changed.
- **375 and desktop:** checked at 375×812, 768×1024, 1024×768 and 1280×800, and at DPR 2 at full strength.

**How I did it**
- **Nothing per pixel per frame.** All the motion comes from cached textures:
  - **Cells:** a small seamless tile of 8×8 cells, baked per pixel as a multiplicatively weighted Voronoi pattern. Bright cells have smaller distances, so they swell and squeeze their neighbors. Each pixel is shaded from the ratio of its two nearest distances: rounded bright middles, dark lanes where cells meet. There are 8 tiles, one per step of an 8 s boil loop.
  - Per frame, the disk shows two neighboring steps as canvas patterns. They crossfade with cos/sin weights, which keeps the texture contrast steady, and the pattern drifts slowly. The tiles are baked at device resolution, up to 2×, so the cells stay crisp at DPR 2.
  - **Swells:** one larger soft tile (1.7 r × 2.1 r) of bright and dark blobs, stamped with wrapped copies so it repeats without seams. It drifts as a pattern.
  - Cells and swells are three `fill()`s of one disk path. Each pattern's transform is set through one reused `DOMMatrix`, with offsets snapped to device pixels.
  - **Masking without a per-frame mask:** the corona sprite became an "overlay". Inside the disk, the corona is cleared and replaced with the disk's own limb colors, from transparent at 0.62 r to opaque at the edge, plus the sunspots. Drawn over the surface layers, it fades the cells out toward the limb, as the old granulation mask did. It is the same size and has the same cost as the old corona draw, and it still breathes.
  - Per frame that is disk + 3 fills + overlay, against disk + 2 granulation blits + corona before, so one extra disk-sized fill.
- **Bursts:** at most four small sprite draws while a burst runs:
  - the flash: a glow and a hot core, drawn additively;
  - the eruption sprite, rotated to the burst site and anchored at the limb;
  - the clump running ahead.
  - The eruption sprites (one jet, one loop) are baked once per size. Each has room under its feet, so its glow is never cut off by the sprite's edge. My first version showed a sharp edge there, which I fixed.
  - At first the jet lifted off as a whole and looked like a thrown stick. Now it stays anchored and stretches outward.
  - The eruption uses `source-over`, not additive blending, which was a bit cheaper in the micro-benchmark.
- **Schedule:** the next burst (start, length, place as a 0–1 fraction of the visible arc, kind, flip) is planned with `Math.random`. The plan sits in one small object, so `draw` allocates nothing. Storing the place as a fraction keeps it valid after a resize.
  - I added a module-level hand-off. When V0.61's sharp-mode rebuild creates a new instance at the same layer time (within 1 s), the new instance adopts the schedule, so a burst in progress carries on. A fresh visit starts at time 0 and gets a new schedule.
- **Test hook:** `window.__sunBurstTest = { every, steps }` forces bursts every `every` seconds (at least 5), stepping along the visible arc in `steps` places, jets first and then loops. It is compiled in only when `import.meta.env.DEV` is true or the build has `VITE_SUN_TEST=1`. A normal `npm run build` removes it as dead code. I checked that `__sunBurstTest` appears 0 times in `dist/assets/*.js`, and once in the test build.
- **WCAG 2.3.1:** each burst has one flash, a single smooth rise and fall over 2 s, and bursts are at least 20 s apart. That is at most one flash per 20 s, against the limit of 3 per second. The flash is a patch about 0.5 r across, never the whole screen, and with content shown the canvas is still capped at 0.2 opacity. The boil crossfade is 1 Hz and smooth, and changes the colour of each pixel only a little; it is not a flash.
- **Bake / first frame:** the old bake stamped up to 2×5000 granulation dots, which was the bulk of the sun's build time. The new cell tiles are about 70×70 px each at 375 (about 120 px at 1280) and are baked per pixel, with a yield every ~3000 pixels. The total bake stays about the same or gets faster (numbers below).
- **Ambiguity I resolved myself:** "never two at once" is guaranteed by the gap (≥ 20 s) being longer than the burst (≤ 5 s). The test hook also enforces a gap of at least one burst length.
