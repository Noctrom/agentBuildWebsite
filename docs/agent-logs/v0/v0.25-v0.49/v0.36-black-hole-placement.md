**What I did**
- At tablet widths (768–1023px) the Behind the Scenes black hole now sits higher and further right. It is in the top-right corner, beside the page title and above the end of the intro. At the V0.32 spot (the "Branches and reviews" heading 140px from the top), it is clear of the last intro lines and of the first branch-diagram box. - 1280 and 1024 are unchanged: the before and after screenshots are pixel-identical. At 375 the still frame moved by under 1px. When scrolled with motion on, the hole lifts 8px instead of 40px, so its shadow stays below the header band (see below).
- Kept the important parts (the shadow and the photon ring) below the ~65px header band that the V0.35 sticky header will cover. This holds for phones and tablets, also after the parallax lift. Desktop already cleared it (shadow top ≥ 132px when scrolled).
- `src/scenes/routes.ts` comment now describes the V0.33 behaviour: the old scene fades out at once, the new one fades in when ready, and with reduce motion the swap is instant. The word "crossfades" is gone.
- AA contrast holds and per-frame cost is unchanged (see Verification).

**How I did it**
- `layoutFor` gained a tablet band (`768 <= width < 1024`), where `cx = width - 1.75r` and `cy = 65 + lift + 1.5r`. That places the hole from the header band down, not as a fraction of height. On tall portrait tablets a height fraction put it right on the intro.
- The fixed `MAX_PARALLAX = 40` became a per-layout `lift`: 8 on phones, 16 on tablets, 40 on desktop (unchanged). With a 40px lift, the 375 shadow slid halfway under the header after scrolling, which would get worse with a sticky header.
- New constant `HEADER_BAND = 65` (the measured header height below lg).
- I tried `cy = 65 + lift + 1.9r` first. It was clear of the box but still sat on the first two intro lines, so I raised it to 1.5r.
- Draw code is untouched: the same sprites and the same draws per frame. Smaller tablet sprites only make the draws cheaper.
- Trade-off at 375: with the smaller lift, the scrolled hole sits ~32px lower than before. At the bottom of the page that puts it behind the "Merged by the leader after review" label (animated p95 6.49 → 5.64, lowest single pixel 5.27 → 5.26). That is still well above 4.5, and it is the same spot reduce-motion users already see.
