# V0.68 — Contact galaxy moves down toward the bottom-right corner on tablet and desktop (2026-10-07)
**Agent:** dev-2
**Branch:** story/v0.68-galaxy-corner · **Status:** done

**What I did**
- Tablet and desktop (768px and wider): the galaxy now sits low in the bottom-right corner. Its centre is lower and further right than before at all four sizes (table below).
- The whole swept outline (every angle of the turn) stays on screen. It keeps a gap of 32–72 px to the right edge and 21–27 px to the footer's controls. The screen bottom is 85–91 px away.
- Same size: `r` is computed exactly as before.
- The companion galaxy and the sky tint with its fine and bright stars still follow the galaxy's `cx`/`cy`, so they move with it. The companion sits up and left of the disk as before. The tint still surrounds the galaxy; its lower part now runs off screen.
- The Milky Way band stays where it was. On desktop it has its own anchor, set to the galaxy's old place. Its glow, dust and fine stars are pixel-identical left of the tint (checked at 1280×800). The galaxy is still drawn in front of it.
- The galaxy never covers the contact text, the buttons or the footer text. These pages don't scroll at the four sizes, and the galaxy only moves up when scrolling (parallax), so scroll 0 is the worst case for the footer. AA: 0 fails, and the lowest values are the same as on leader.
- Swirl, core breathing, twinkle, colours and the hidden-content view: their code is unchanged. Only the desktop `layoutFor` branch and the desktop band origin changed.
- Phones (<768px) and the other pages are pixel-identical to leader (reduced-motion frames, see Verification).

**How I did it**
- New desktop layout in `layoutFor` (`src/scenes/galaxy.ts`):
  - `r = max(150, min(0.2 w, 0.36 h))` (unchanged)
  - `cx = w - max(24, 0.025 w) - 1.0 r` (`SWEEP_HALF_WIDTH = 1`)
  - `cy = h - 64 - 16 - 0.64 r` (footer top `DESKTOP_FOOTER_TOP = 64`, gap `DESKTOP_FOOTER_GAP = 16`, `SWEEP_HALF_HEIGHT` as before)
- Rotation envelope: the disk turns inside its own plane, so the swept outline is the tilted disk's ellipse.
  - Its half-height is the existing measured `SWEEP_HALF_HEIGHT = 0.64 r`.
  - Its half-width follows from the ellipse's aspect (TILT 0.48, PLANE_ANGLE −0.42): 0.934 / 0.599 × 0.64 ≈ 1.0 r.
  - Measured over a full 92 s turn, at ≥ 6/255 on screen: 0.93–0.95 r wide and 0.60–0.62 r high at every size. So both constants leave a little extra on top of the gaps.
- Footer limit: on /contact the eye button's box starts 64 px above the viewport bottom at 768×1024, 1024×768, 1280×800 and 1920×1080; the © glyphs start at 56. I used the button box (the highest footer control) as the limit. The pages don't scroll at these sizes. On a page that does scroll, the footer only reaches `h - 64` at the end of the scroll, while the galaxy only moves up, so the limit holds there too.
- Right gap: `max(24, 2.5% of the width)`. At 768 the galaxy is width-limited (r = 0.2 w), so a fixed 32 px gap would have put `cx` slightly left of the old 0.76 w. 24 px keeps it right of the old place. 2.5% grows the gap a little on wide screens, so the corner doesn't look cramped at 1920.
- Band: in `galaxyBand.ts`, `bandShape` now places the desktop band from `desktopAnchor(w, h) = (0.76 w, 0.46 h)`, the galaxy's old centre, instead of from the galaxy. Its sizes still scale with `r` (unchanged), so the band is the same.
  - The band's twinkling stars still avoid the spiral's current disk (it is drawn in front), so a few of them are placed differently: 87 changed pixels at 1920×1080 left of the tint, 0 at 1280×800.
  - The phone path is untouched.
- Sky tint (`skyShape`): unchanged formula, so it moves with the galaxy. Its bottom now runs off screen, and it lies behind the right end of the footer. The AA scan shows no change in the footer's lowest contrast, so I left it.
- Measuring problem: the footprint method (per-pixel min/max over a turn) also picks up the starfield's drifting nebula next to the galaxy at 1280×800. I kept only the connected region around the centre, clipped to 1.2× the disk ellipse. At 1280 I also excluded columns left of x = 820, where the drift streak was; the ellipse's lowest point is at x ≈ 861, so it is still included.
