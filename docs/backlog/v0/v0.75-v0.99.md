## V0.75 — Soft starfield clouds without banding
Requested by Chris directly to the leader (2026-10-17, loose-ends list). Owner: dev-2 (`src/scenes/starfield.ts`). **Re-scoped 2026-10-17 by the leader after dev-2's investigation:** the banding from the V0.61 log (Home, `hidden-1280-dpr2-home.png`) is not in `nebula.ts` (the /resume nebula is already dithered and clean). It comes from the soft clouds in `starfield.ts`: they are drawn with `createRadialGradient` into a quarter-size bitmap (`NEBULA_SCALE = 0.25`) and stretched to the screen, which magnifies the browser's gradient dither into a regular ~4 CSS px checker pattern, 5× more visible at full strength. These clouds appear on Home, About, Projects, Contact, Behind the Scenes and 404. Fix: bake the cloud bitmaps in code (same blobs, falloff and dust holes) with random dither, the way `nebula.ts` bakes its layers, or another approach that measures as well. No change to `backgroundEngine.ts` (dev-1) is expected; if one is needed, report it.

*As a visitor who hides the content to look at the background, I want smooth clouds so that the view looks polished.*

- [ ] No visible checker or blocky banding in the starfield clouds at full strength (hidden view), Home at 1280 DPR 2 and 375 (before/after zooms in the log)
- [ ] The normal (content shown) view looks unchanged on all six pages that use the starfield clouds (before/after screenshots of each at 1280, and Home at 375)
- [ ] Per-frame cost does not rise (frame time at 4× throttle, 375 and 1280, before and after, alternated); the one-time bake per resize is reported and adds no long tasks
- [ ] Reduced motion still shows a correct still background
- [ ] Works at 375px and desktop widths
